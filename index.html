06 / Engineering story

Designed around streaming reliability.

“When millions of viewers press play at the same time, a small service degradation can become a customer-facing incident. StreamPulse explores how engineering teams can see the full system, detect anomalies early and trace failures across distributed services.”

The engineering problem: telemetry is generated by many independent services, and fragmented monitoring makes it difficult to connect a latency spike to its underlying cause.

The proposed solution: standardize telemetry with OpenTelemetry, route events through a resilient pipeline, store signals for fast analysis, and surface correlated dashboards and actionable alerts.

What to demonstrate in an interview: ingestion design, Kafka partition strategy, consumer backpressure, idempotency, time-series retention, trace context propagation, anomaly thresholds, SLOs and failure recovery.
